# Code of Conduct

Xyron Center is a small project with a simple rule: **be decent to each other.**

## Expected

- Be welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so everyone can follow.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
